About Tickwell

Tickwell is a simple and free study timer designed to help you focus on your tasks and organize your study time.

With a clean and easy-to-use interface, Tickwell lets you start a study session, track your time, and stay focused without unnecessary distractions.

Why Tickwell?

Tickwell was created to make studying easier and more organized. Whether you are studying for school, university, or working on personal projects, you can use Tickwell to manage your time and maintain your focus.

Simple. Focused. Free.

Tickwell focuses on providing the essential tools you need for a productive study session while keeping the experience simple and distraction-free.
