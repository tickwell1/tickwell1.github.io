How to Use Tickwell

Tickwell is designed to make studying simple and focused. Follow these steps to start using the timer.

1. Start the Timer

Open Tickwell and press the Start button to begin your study session.

2. Focus on Your Work

Once the timer starts, focus on your study, reading, homework, or any task you want to complete.

3. Pause the Timer

Press the Stop button whenever you want to pause your study session.

4. Reset the Timer

Press the Reset button to return the timer to 00:00:00 and start a new session.

5. Change the Theme

Use the theme button to switch between Dark Mode and Bright Mode according to your preference.

6. Use Fullscreen Mode

Press the fullscreen button to make the timer fill the screen and reduce distractions while studying.

Study Tips

Choose a specific task before starting the timer and try to stay focused until your study session is finished.

You can use Tickwell for short study sessions or longer periods depending on your schedule.
