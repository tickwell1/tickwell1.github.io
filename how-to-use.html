How to Use Tickwell

Tickwell is designed to make studying simple and focused. Follow these steps to start using the timer.

1. Start the Timer

Open Tickwell and press the Start button to begin your study session.

2. Focus on Your Work

Once the timer starts, focus on your study, reading, homework, or any task you want to complete.

3. Pause the Timer

Press the Stop button whenever you want to pause your study session.

4. Reset the Timer

Press the Reset button to return the timer to 00:00:00 and start a new session.

5. Change the Theme

Use the theme button to switch between Dark Mode and Bright Mode according to your preference.

6. Use Fullscreen Mode

Press the fullscreen button to make the timer fill the screen and reduce distractions while studying.

7. Keyboard Shortcuts

On a computer, you can control Tickwell from the keyboard: Space to start or pause, Shift + R to reset, F for fullscreen, T to change the theme, M to switch between Stopwatch and Pomodoro, and ? to show the list of shortcuts.

8. Pomodoro Mode

Switch to Pomodoro mode with the toggle above the timer. Tickwell counts down your focus time (45 minutes by default) and then your break. A chime plays when focus ends, and an alarm rings at the end of the break until you press I'm here. You can change the durations in Settings.

9. Dashboard & Daily Goal

Open the Dashboard from the menu to see your study time for today, this week and this month, your streak of consecutive days, and your progress toward the daily goal you set.

10. Settings & Sync

In Settings you can switch between Arabic and English, choose a dark, light or automatic theme, adjust sounds and notifications, and install Tickwell as an app. Log in with your email to sync your statistics across all your devices.

Study Tips

Choose a specific task before starting the timer and try to stay focused until your study session is finished.

You can use Tickwell for short study sessions or longer periods depending on your schedule.
