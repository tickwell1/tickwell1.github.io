Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Tickwell handles information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

You can use the main timer without creating an account. If you choose to create an account, we collect the email address you register with, and your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). If you sign in, your study statistics and some preferences (language, theme, Pomodoro durations, sound settings, and the subject names you create) are stored in your account using Google Cloud Firestore so they can sync across your devices, and you can delete your account and all synced data at any time from the Settings page. If you send a message through the Contact page, your name, email address, and message are sent to us through Formspree.

Local Storage

Tickwell uses your browser's local storage to remember your preferences (such as your theme), your timer state, and your study statistics (session durations, daily goal, and Pomodoro counts). If you are not signed in, this information stays only on your device and is not sent to us. If you sign in, it is also synced to your account as described above. Clearing your browser data removes the local copy, and signing out removes your statistics from the device (they remain in your account). Tickwell also stores copies of its own files in your browser so it can work offline.

Cookies

Tickwell does not require cookies for the basic operation of the study timer. Google Analytics, which we use to understand how the website is used, is loaded only if you accept it in the cookie notice, and it may set cookies in your browser. You can change your choice at any time on the Settings page.

Third-Party Services

Tickwell uses Google Analytics to measure website usage, Firebase Authentication to provide optional email sign-in, Google Cloud Firestore to store synced data for signed-in users, and Formspree to deliver messages sent through the Contact form. These services have their own privacy policies. If we add other services in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Children's Privacy

Tickwell is a general-purpose study timer and does not knowingly require children to provide personal information to use the basic features of the website.

Data Security

We aim to keep Tickwell simple and minimize the amount of information required to use the website. Since no website or online service can guarantee complete security, users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if Tickwell's features or services change. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Tickwell, you can contact us through the Contact page.
