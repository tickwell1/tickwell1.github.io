Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Tickwell handles information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Tickwell is designed to be a simple study timer. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the main timer features.

Local Storage

Tickwell may use your browser's local storage to remember certain preferences, such as your selected theme. This information is stored on your device and is not directly sent to us.

Cookies

Tickwell does not currently require cookies for the basic operation of the study timer.

Third-Party Services

Tickwell may use third-party services in the future. If third-party services are added, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain what information those services may collect and how they are used.

Children's Privacy

Tickwell is a general-purpose study timer and does not knowingly require children to provide personal information to use the basic features of the website.

Data Security

We aim to keep Tickwell simple and minimize the amount of information required to use the website. Since no website or online service can guarantee complete security, users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if Tickwell's features or services change. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Tickwell, you can contact us through the Contact page.
